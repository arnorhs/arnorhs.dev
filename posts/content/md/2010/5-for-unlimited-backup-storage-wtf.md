_I'm sorry if this post sounds like an advertisement, **but it is not.**_ I ran into this backup service yesterday. It's called Backblaze I was pretty surprised when I saw the price... $5 a month for a complete backup of your computer.. that is: unlimited storage for $5 a month. That's nothing short of amazing. I'm always looking for backup solutions, so this sounded good, but maybe a bit too good. But it seems kind of scary to trust your data to $5 backup service. A user called barmstrong on HN [pointed me](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=1639532) to this blog post by Backblaze on how they build their servers and therefor how they are able to do storage so cheaply. [Petabytes on a budget: How to build cheap cloud storage | Backblaze Blog](https://blog.backblaze.com/2009/09/01/petabytes-on-a-budget-how-to-build-cheap-cloud-storage/).
