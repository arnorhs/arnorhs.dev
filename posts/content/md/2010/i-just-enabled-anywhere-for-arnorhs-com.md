Twitter recently launched a development platform called @anywhere. You can find more information about that on [https://dev.twitter.com/](https://dev.twitter.com/) (Side note: I really like the design of the site) The service allows for all kinds of integrations of twitter with your website, so now, for instance my twitter handle: @arnorhs becomes clickable, exactly the same way as it does on twitter. I don't know which aspects of this thing I'll be using, but there might be some changes here along the way. @anywhere is basically a competitor for Facebook-connect, which has had tremendous success in the past months. I'm thinking about ditching the comment section on the website all together and opt in for twitter @replies instead. What do you think?
