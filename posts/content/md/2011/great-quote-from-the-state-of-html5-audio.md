I was reading through a blog post titled "The state of HTML5 audio" and I found it very amusing. Warning.. There is profanity. Read at your own risk. The best bit was this one:

> "Browsers From Companies That Hate the Web Enough to Not Support Ogg/Vorbis, but do Have an <Audio> Tag So They Can Say They Have an <Audio> Tag Seriously, Fuck You" Wohoo, big surprise, its Apple and Microsoft! Who wouldve thought?

via [The State of HTML5 Audio - PhobosLab](https://www.phoboslab.org/log/2011/03/the-state-of-html5-audio). But the criticism is very valid. I hadn't tried using <audio> tags at all but from reading this post I'm pretty disappointed with Chrome, which is currently my preferred browser.
