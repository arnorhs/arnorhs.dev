A new Hacker News-style social news site has been launched focused exclusively around Javascript. It's a nice source for some interesting JS libraries and articles. It's built on the [Lamer News](https://lamernews.com/) [source code](https://github.com/antirez/lamernews). If you are passionate about Javascript, you should check it out: [https://www.echojs.com/](https://www.echojs.com/)
